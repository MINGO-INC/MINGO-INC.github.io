How it works

Four steps. Two days.

No meetings. No slide decks. Just a conversation about what's eating your time, and then we fix it.

01

You tell us what's broken

Describe the process that's driving you crazy. We listen. We ask questions. We figure out if we can help. If not, we tell you honestly.

Within 24 hours, you get a one-page document showing exactly what we'll automate, how it'll work, and what you'll get. You approve it or we revise.
